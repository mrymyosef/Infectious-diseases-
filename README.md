# Infectious-diseases-
🚨 New Study Alert! 🚨
Our comprehensive analysis over eight years in Saudi Arabia reveals significant variations in notifiable diseases linked to regional influences like climate and healthcare infrastructure. 🌍🔍

Critical trends and patterns have been uncovered, emphasizing the urgent need for targeted enhancements in food safety measures and public health strategies. 🛡️🍽️

This study delves deep into disease dynamics, offering actionable insights crucial for crafting effective measures to prevent foodborne illnesses and enhance health outcomes in the region. 🌟📈

Join us as we dissect these findings and discuss how they can shape the future of public health in Saudi Arabia! 

•	Purpose: To investigate the prevalence, temporal trends, and regional distribution of notifiable infectious and foodborne diseases across Saudi Arabia.
•	Tools Used: R, SPSS, and Excel (for descriptive statistics, Spearman correlation, Kruskal–Wallis, Mann–Whitney, regression, and time-series visualization).
•	Main Tasks: Cleaned and structured national surveillance data; analyzed disease prevalence by region, season, and demographic factors; conducted correlation and non-parametric tests; and developed time-series and geospatial visualizations.
•	Results & Impact: Identified Brucellosis, Amoebic Dysentery, and Salmonella as the most common infections, with significant seasonal and regional patterns. Findings support targeted public health interventions and food safety strategies in high-risk regions.
